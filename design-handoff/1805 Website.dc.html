Weddings

Punjabi, Sikh and Indian weddings across Vancouver, Surrey, Abbotsford, the Fraser Valley and wherever your celebration takes you.

The approach

Every tradition, as it naturally unfolds

Weddings are a beautiful whirlwind of traditions, emotions and family coming together. From the laughter of the Maiyan and Mehndi to the anticipation before the Anand Karaj and the energy of the reception, I document every part of the celebration as it naturally unfolds.

I capture the big moments you expect, along with the quiet in-between ones you might miss in the rush: the happy tears, stolen glances, and all the joy that makes your wedding uniquely yours.

Why a former makeup artist

I spent over a decade in the bridal suite as a makeup artist. I know the rhythm of a Punjabi wedding morning, I know when the chunni goes on and when mom cries, and I know how to stay out of the way while catching all of it.

Your getting-ready photos are the ones I've been preparing for the longest.

The girl behind the camera

Meet Manmeet Mandair.

I'm probably fixing your hair mid-shoot and telling you you look good every five minutes.

For over a decade I worked as a makeup artist in the wedding industry, always close to moments filled with emotion, anticipation and connection. I witnessed countless love stories unfold: the quiet nerves before the ceremony, the joy, the tears, and everything in between.

But it wasn't until I became a mother that I truly understood how quickly time moves. After having my daughter, I found myself wanting to capture every fleeting stage, the tiny details and the everyday moments I never wanted to forget. That desire is what led to 1805 Photography.

Now good light, real feelings and the moments that happen when no one's trying too hard, that's my thing. From popping the question to chasing kids around, I document it all in a way that feels easy, real and completely you.
