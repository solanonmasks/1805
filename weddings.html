The approach

Every tradition, as it naturally unfolds

Weddings are a beautiful whirlwind of traditions, emotions and family coming together. From the laughter of the Maiyan and Mehndi to the anticipation before the Anand Karaj and the energy of the reception, I document every part of the celebration as it naturally unfolds.

I capture the big moments you expect, along with the quiet in-between ones you might miss in the rush: the happy tears, stolen glances, and all the joy that makes your wedding uniquely yours.

Why a former makeup artist

I spent over a decade in the bridal suite as a makeup artist. I know the rhythm of a Punjabi wedding morning, I know when the chunni goes on and when mom cries, and I know how to stay out of the way while catching all of it.

Your getting-ready photos are the ones I've been preparing for the longest.

Questions couples ask

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely. I love documenting love stories in new places. Clients cover travel costs outside the Lower Mainland.

How do we book?

Fill out the contact form with your event details and I'll reply within 24 to 48 hours with availability. A signed contract and non-refundable deposit secure your date.

What is your rescheduling policy?

Your deposit transfers to a new date pending availability. If a new date cannot be arranged, the deposit remains non-refundable.

When do we get our photos?

Wedding galleries are delivered within two to three months through a private online gallery you can view, download and share. Expedited editing is available for an additional fee.

Will all photos be edited?

Every image in your final gallery is fully edited in my signature style. Raw files are not provided.

Is there a travel fee?

Sessions within the Fraser Valley are included. Outside the Fraser Valley there is a $50 travel fee.
